# Article Interview Question Bank

Use these questions during Stage 1 (Discovery) based on the article type you're working on.
Don't ask all of them — pick the ones most likely to unlock the writer's best thinking.

---

## Universal Opener Questions
(Use these first for any article type)

- If you had to explain this to a smart friend who doesn't work in tech, what would you say?
- What made you want to write about this specifically? Was there a moment of frustration, surprise, or excitement?
- What's the thing about this topic that you wish someone had told you a year ago?
- Who is the ideal reader for this piece? What's their job title and what problem are they trying to solve?
- What do you want readers to feel when they finish — informed? Relieved? Motivated? Skeptical?

---

## How-To / Tutorial Articles

- What's the prerequisite knowledge you're assuming the reader has?
- What are the top 3 mistakes people make when doing this for the first time?
- Is there a faster / simpler path that most tutorials skip?
- What does "done" look like? Is there a clear success state?
- Are there gotchas in the setup that will cause people to fail silently?
- What would you do differently if you were starting over?
- What version / environment are you targeting? Will this go stale?

---

## Opinion / Takes Articles

- What's your actual thesis? Can you say "I believe X because Y"?
- Who disagrees with you, and what's the strongest version of their argument?
- What evidence do you have — is this gut feel, personal experience, or data?
- Have you changed your mind on this? What shifted?
- Is this a minority opinion in your field, or mainstream? Does that matter?
- What would need to be true for you to be wrong?
- Is there nuance you're leaving out for the sake of argument? Should you include it?

---

## Architecture Deep-Dive Articles

- What's the problem this architecture is solving? What failure mode does it prevent?
- What did the architecture look like before? What drove the change?
- Where are the seams — the interfaces between components that are most likely to cause problems?
- What are the scaling limits? Where does this break down?
- What did you have to unlearn from previous systems to design this well?
- What trade-offs did you consciously make? What did you give up?
- If someone wanted to implement this tomorrow, what would trip them up first?
- Are there competing architectural patterns? Why did you pick this one?

---

## Case Study / Lessons Learned Articles

- Tell me the story. Walk me through what happened, start to finish.
- What was the moment you realized something was wrong (or right)?
- What did you try that didn't work before you found what did?
- What would you do differently with the benefit of hindsight?
- What's the insight that only someone who lived through this would have?
- Is there a number that illustrates the impact — time saved, error rate reduced, cost change?
- What assumption turned out to be wrong?
- Would this lesson generalize to other teams / companies / situations?

---

## Comparison / Tradeoffs Articles

- Are you comparing these fairly — same use case, same constraints?
- What context makes Option A the clear winner? What context flips it to Option B?
- What do most comparison articles get wrong about this?
- Is there a "trap" that makes one option look better than it is?
- Which would you personally recommend, and under what conditions?
- Is there a hybrid approach that most people overlook?
- Who is each option actually built for?
- What's the total cost of ownership consideration that's usually missing from these comparisons?

---

## Microsoft / Enterprise Tech Articles

- How does this fit into the broader Microsoft ecosystem? What does it integrate with?
- Is this a "greenfield" technology or an evolution of something older (and does that legacy matter)?
- What does the licensing / pricing structure mean for who can actually use this?
- How does this behave in a real enterprise environment (governance, compliance, security)?
- Where is Microsoft's documentation misleading or incomplete?
- What's the adoption friction — what do teams need to change to use this?
- Is there a notable difference between the "demo experience" and the "production experience"?
- What questions should an architect ask before committing to this technology?
